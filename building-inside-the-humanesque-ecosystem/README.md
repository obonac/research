# Building Inside the Humanesque Ecosystem

A practical builder brief for anyone creating software, research, tooling, or other artifacts within or alongside the Humanesque ecosystem. The brief introduces Humanesque as a constituted epistemic, intellectual, and technological domain with its own contract, tooling, provenance chain, and publishing infrastructure, and establishes the governing principle of Sovereignty without Suzerainty: recognition is not submission, non-participation is not denial, and disagreement is not disrespect. It directs builders to retrieve authoritative governance material before building; avoid rebuilding existing infrastructure; use provider-neutral, non-hardcoded, execution-verified, provenance-aware workflows; apply gated irreversibility; distinguish canonical mirrors from working copies and filesystem visibility from provenance; and use the existing Zistgah, ILM, FAKIR, PEDLER, kitab, matba, dome, mez, Misty DOI, tok-doi, and Proclamation of Individual Equity infrastructure. It also specifies sealing, OpenTimestamps, DOI minting, versioning, licensing, attribution, contribution, and counter-observation practices. Its governing rule is: Mint your own work. Attribute what is not yours.
